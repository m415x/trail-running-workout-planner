## Delivered behavior

H1 introduces temporal `TeamEconomicPolicy`, temporal `AthleteBillingTerms` and immutable `MonthlyCharge` snapshots with equivalent SQLite/Supabase persistence. Team defaults are prospective configuration; athlete terms preserve the condition actually applied; monthly materialization is idempotent and explicit.

The Coach UI adds localized ES/EN membership management. Team policy and athlete terms are shown as current/scheduled/history timelines. Existing materialized charges are read-only on the athlete surface; page navigation does not materialize charges.

## Durable invariants

- Money uses integer minor units. The initial ARS 25,000 / day-5 values are configuration, not universal constants.
- Team policies are temporal and month-boundary based.
- Initial athlete terms may start any day; ordinary replacements are prospective month-boundary changes.
- Any reached portion of a month produces the full monthly fee; no H1 proration.
- Only one billing-terms interval may claim an athlete/month; persistence uniquely protects `(athleteId, year, month)`.
- Materialized charges are historical snapshots and are not rewritten by later prospective changes.
- Materialization is an application operation independent of UI navigation.
- Legacy `memberships` is preserved but is not economic authority; ambiguous legacy rows are not automatically converted into economic history.
- Team/athlete isolation is checked at application/persistence boundaries.
- H1 does not implement H2+ reductions, due-date exceptions/extensions, payments, derived account status/balance, notices or Athlete blocking.

The durable domain contract is `docs/architecture/memberships.md`.
