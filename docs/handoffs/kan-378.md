# KAN-378 — Versioned individual execution guidance handoff

Status: complete and integrated into `dev` via PR #11.

## Delivered behavior

KAN-378 separates prescription intent from athlete-specific execution guidance.

- Quality sessions keep an explicit coach percentage (`pam_percentage`) distinct from Z1–Z5. Execution pace/speed is derived from the canonical KAN-377 1000 m `RunningReference`; the percentage does not assert that the test directly measures physiological PAM/MAS.
- Missing RunningReference remains `unknown`; no quality pace is synthesized.
- Z1–Z5 execution guidance is independent from the 1000 m reference and exposes first-class RPE and Talk Test cues for all five zones.
- Trail guidance explicitly prioritizes effort over pace.
- Heart-rate reference resolution prefers valid known evidence. If known max HR is absent and birthday is valid, Tanaka 2001 (`208 - 0.7 × age`) provides an explicitly `age_predicted` max-HR reference at the effective date.
- Birthday never estimates resting HR. Missing resting HR remains `unknown`.
- Karvonen requires explicit valid resting HR. With max HR but no resting HR, zone BPM calculation uses %HRmax rather than fabricated HRR.
- No heart-rate value is derived from the 1000 m test.

The guidance contracts are versioned and preserve source/provenance where evidence is available.

## Application boundary

The legacy WorkoutCard boundary did not carry the discriminated `TrainingIntensity`. It previously inferred pace from `workout.zone` through `ZONE_PAM_PERCENTAGES` and fabricated BPM guidance with hard-coded 190/50 values.

KAN-378 removes both behaviors:

- WorkoutCard no longer derives pace from an HR zone or accepts `athletePamSec` for that purpose.
- WorkoutCard no longer displays fabricated BPM guidance.
- Existing planned `workout.time` and `workout.pace` remain the presentation values until an explicit intensity/guidance contract reaches this boundary.

Transporting `TrainingIntensity` / resolved `ExecutionGuidance` through `WorkoutProps` is deferred rather than inventing data in this story.

## Persistence and security

No database schema, migration, repository ownership rule or RLS policy changed in KAN-378. Guidance is resolved in the domain/application layer and does not persist a parallel physiology authority or mutate accepted planning.

## Acceptance audit

- Z2 remains executable without a sensor or measured HR through RPE + Talk Test.
- Prescription and execution guidance are discriminated.
- Without a 1000 m reference, zone/RPE/Talk Test and available HR evidence remain independent.
- Known HR evidence preserves provenance and takes precedence over age prediction.
- Missing known max HR plus valid birthday produces an explicitly estimated Tanaka reference.
- Missing reliable resting HR never falls back to 50 and never fabricates Karvonen/HRR.
- Guidance identifies policy version and evidence/formula provenance.
- Age is calculated from birthday at the effective date rather than persisted as mutable state.
- Five zones, measured-vs-age-predicted HR, unknowns, policy version and quality-percentage behavior are covered by focused tests.

## Deferred debt

Legacy `physiology_records` / `AthletePhysiology` still model max/rest HR as required and mix older PAM/1000 m concepts with cardiac values. KAN-378 does not treat that structure as new physiology authority and does not expand scope into a persistence migration.

Legacy `ZONE_PAM_PERCENTAGES` and `getZonePaceRangeFromPam` may remain for compatibility elsewhere, but they are not authority for the KAN-378 execution policy and WorkoutCard no longer consumes the mapping.

The next integration that exposes individualized guidance in WorkoutCard must transport an explicit `TrainingIntensity` and authorized evidence/reference rather than reconstructing it from `zone`.
