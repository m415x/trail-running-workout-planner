## KAN-462 acceptance audit

KAN-462 delivers a deterministic, auditable account projection from persisted H1/H2/H3 facts without introducing mutable H4 economic authority.

- Each persisted charge derives one of `settled | pending | overdue`.
- `remainingMinor === 0` derives `settled`.
- With a positive remaining balance, `cutoffDate <= effectiveDueDate` derives `pending`.
- With a positive remaining balance, `cutoffDate > effectiveDueDate` derives `overdue`.
- `effectiveDueDate` is inclusive.
- The cutoff date is an explicit civil `YYYY-MM-DD` input; H4 domain derivation does not read the runtime clock or timezone.
- Calendar day 10 does not create or alter an economic state.
- Paid and remaining amounts reuse the H3 current-non-void Payment projection.
- Aggregate balances are grouped by persisted currency; unlike monetary units are never summed or implicitly converted.
- No H4 balance, account status, debt state or economic-history ledger is persisted as mutable authority.

## Explainable economic history

For each persisted MonthlyCharge, H4 exposes a structured explanation relating:

- applicable AthleteBillingTerms;
- persisted MonthlyCharge;
- global due-date exception revision history;
- reduction/scholarship revision history;
- individual extension revision history;
- Payment correction/void revision history;
- the final derived H4 result at the supplied cutoff date.

The explanation preserves H2/H3 revision authority and distinguishes historical, current and voided facts. It deliberately does not invent a total chronological ledger where source facts do not provide one homogeneous chronology.

## Read-model integration

The athlete Membership page loader now accepts `cutoffDate` separately from general `onDate`.

It gathers persisted charges, billing terms, global due-date exceptions, reductions, extensions and Payment revisions under the existing team + athlete isolation boundary, then returns:

- `accountState`
- `economicHistory`

The read path remains side-effect-free: it does not materialize charges or mutate any H1/H2/H3 fact.

## Coach ES/EN delivery

The Coach athlete Membership accordion now displays:

- derived account state per charge;
- amount due, paid and remaining;
- total remaining balance grouped by currency;
- effective due date;
- explainable economic history for condition, global exception, reduction/scholarship, extension, payments and derived result.

The presentation consumes H4 projections and formats only dates/money. It does not derive status or balances in the UI.

Currency codes remain explicit alongside localized amounts so multi-currency account projections are not visually ambiguous.

H4 copy is localized ES/EN. The economic history is open by default and remains collapsible for Coach auditability.

## H5 boundary

KAN-462 does not implement Athlete debt signaling, notices or prior-debt blocking.

KAN-463 owns:

- Athlete `pending` yellow signaling;
- Athlete `overdue` red signaling;
- normal `settled` presentation;
- derived `blocked_for_prior_debt` when a positive balance exists in a prior calendar month;
- removal of the signal/block after payment settles the relevant debt;
- remaining Coach/Athlete experience reconciliation against the already implemented economic domain.

H5 must consume H4 projections rather than persist a parallel debt/blocking authority. This remains Membership application behavior and does not introduce KAN-298 identity/session/role/authorization concerns.
