## Delivered contract

KAN-604 applies the completed H1/H2/H3 identity/authentication/authorization stack to Coach-side AthleteProfile and Sporting Group administration.

### Active Team

- H2 remains userId-only.
- Active Team is server-validated operational context, never authorization authority by itself.
- Cookie: `ept_active_team`; Team id only; `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Path=/`, secure in production.
- Exactly one applicable TeamMembership may auto-resolve.
- Multiple applicable memberships without validated selection fail closed.
- Persisted stale/malformed/inapplicable context fails closed.
- Explicit Team selection validates the authenticated User's applicable TeamMembership before writing the cookie.
- Logout clears the active-Team cookie after successful Supabase sign-out.
- No client-selected actor or Team can bypass H3.

### H4A authorization

- Athlete administration capability: `athlete.admin.manage`.
- Sporting Group administration capability: `sporting_group.admin.manage`.
- H4A callers do not check preset names.
- ATHLETE denial and ASSISTANT/COACH/ADMIN applicability come from the H3 capability catalog.
- Resource detail/update/lifecycle derives stored ownership before H3 evaluation.
- Cross-Team identifiers fail closed.
- Team-level list/create operations use the resolved active Team as the TEAM-scoped resource.

### AthleteProfile administration

Protected surfaces include:

- list and detail;
- create;
- edit;
- activation/deactivation;
- active Sporting Group options used by Athlete administration.

Existing H1 boundaries remain authoritative:

- `createAthleteAdministration`;
- `updateAthleteAdministration`;
- `setAthleteProfileActiveState`.

Athlete creation continues to compose billing initialization inside the existing transaction callback; H4A does not rewrite economic-domain persistence.

### Sporting Group administration

Protected surfaces include:

- list and detail;
- create;
- edit/active state.

Direct persistence was extracted from `group-actions.ts` into team-scoped boundaries:

- `createSportingGroupAdministration`;
- `updateSportingGroupAdministration`.

### Mixed Athlete ↔ Sporting Group operations

`getEligibleAthletesForGroup` and `getGroupWithMembers` require both:

- `athlete.admin.manage`;
- `sporting_group.admin.manage`.

Athlete→Sporting Group movement also requires both capabilities before mutation.

The assignment action:

- derives AthleteProfile and destination Sporting Group ownership from stored data;
- rejects missing/cross-Team/inactive destination before mutation;
- derives `changedByUserId` from authenticated H2 `access.userId`;
- delegates the actual mutation to `assignAthleteToGroupSynchronously`, preserving its atomic cohort closure / AthleteProfile update / GroupHistory append behavior.

`returnContext` is navigation-only input. It does not participate in actor, Team, capability, ownership or resource selection and cannot deny an otherwise authorized operation.
