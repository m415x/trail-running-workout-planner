# KAN-479 — Coach ES/EN integration for H2 economic exceptions

## Status

## Delivered behavior

KAN-479 exposes the established H1/H2 Membership contract through localized Coach ES/EN workflows without moving economic rules into the UI.

The Coach Membership surface now supports:

- a global monthly due-date exception for one explicit calendar month, with persisted append-only history;
- reduction/scholarship decisions on persisted monthly charges, including explicit withdrawal;
- individual due-date extensions on persisted monthly charges, including explicit withdrawal;
- localized current/historical audit values for reduction amounts and extension dates;
- explicit team-wide monthly charge materialization for a selected month;
- compact athlete Membership presentation that exposes charge exceptions only when persisted charges exist.

Reduction and extension period identity is derived from the selected persisted charge. Reads and navigation remain read-only.

## H1 materialization and onboarding boundary

Team-wide monthly materialization reuses the canonical H1 materialization contract. It processes eligible team athletes, skips already-existing months and reports only newly inserted charges as created. A future scheduler may call this same application use case instead of introducing a second materialization path.

New athlete creation now initializes Membership atomically when the feature is enabled. Initial `AthleteBillingTerms` come from the team policy effective on the Argentina-local creation date, and the join/current-month charge is materialized in the same synchronous SQLite creation transaction. Creation fails when no policy is effective on that date rather than leaving an economically incomplete athlete.

Join-month materialization applies the current global due-date exception before the first-month activation clamp. Existing legacy athletes without billing terms are not silently backfilled or assigned historical debt.

## Monetary and civil-date presentation audit

A focused pre-closure audit verified the KAN-5 boundary. Persisted `amountMinor` plus `currency` remain the economic authority; locale controls presentation only. Coach amount projections now derive their currency symbol from persisted currency rather than hard-coding `$`, and H2 reduction history no longer invents `ARS` when a charge/currency association is unavailable.

ARS remains the approved initial/default production configuration from KAN-5. Broader team locale/currency/time-zone preferences, global formatter infrastructure and Initial Setup remain deferred rather than being introduced by KAN-479.

`baseDueDate`, `effectiveDueDate`, global exception dates and extension dates remain civil `YYYY-MM-DD` facts. Current UI formatting uses controlled UTC interpretation so presentation cannot shift the stored calendar day.

Remaining locale-format consistency such as common global formatters, locale-specific period formatting and broader regional preferences is presentation debt for KAN-473, not a KAN-460 blocker.
