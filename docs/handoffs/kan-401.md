# KAN-401 — 1000 m TestEvent and lifecycle handoff

Status: implementation and closure gate complete on `kan-380-safe-physiology-coach-athlete-stats`.

## Delivered contract

KAN-401 extends canonical `1000m_track` evidence with durable lifecycle/provenance while preserving the factual field-performance boundary.

- Official evidence is linked to a stable `field_performance_test_events` instance; date alone does not establish officiality.
- Execution context is durable `official | self_directed`. Accepted self-directed evidence remains self-directed.
- Recorder role (`coach | athlete`) and `recorded_by_user_id` are independent provenance dimensions.
- Review lifecycle is `pending_review | accepted | rejected`; self-directed evidence starts pending and only pending active evidence may transition to accepted/rejected.
- Analytical eligibility is derived, not persisted: accepted + active evidence is eligible; legacy rows without lifecycle metadata are accepted by construction.
- Pending, rejected and invalidated evidence do not participate in accepted reference/evolution.
- Corrections remain append-only replacements. Review updates lifecycle metadata only and does not rewrite observed evidence.
- No HR, PAM, MAS, VO2max, readiness or other physiological value is inferred from the 1000 m result.

## Persistence and isolation

SQLite and Supabase persist TestEvent, execution context, recorder provenance and review status. Official lifecycle-aware rows require a TestEvent; self-directed rows cannot reference one. Recorder identity references users without inventing identity for legacy evidence.

Supabase migrations enable RLS for the new TestEvent table and retain RLS for field-performance evidence. The verifier explicitly checks lifecycle columns, application-table coverage and RLS coverage.
