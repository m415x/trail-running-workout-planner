# Training

## Plan adherence
The degree to which confirmed realized outcomes match prescribed sessions. Unknown outcomes reduce evidence coverage rather than counting as failures.

## External load
Work performed by the athlete, represented by externally observable quantities such as distance, duration, and elevation gain.

## Internal load
The athlete's internal response to training. In the current MVP, estimated internal load is represented by session-RPE multiplied by duration.

## Session-RPE (sRPE)
A session-level internal-load method calculated as perceived exertion multiplied by session duration. In this product it is a monitoring input, not a diagnosis of fatigue or injury.

## Planned training
The authoritative prescription resolved for an athlete and date through the planning domain.

## Realized training
The training that was actually performed and recorded. A realized activity may be linked to a planned session or remain `unplanned_realized`.

## Unplanned realized activity
A realized training record without an authoritative planned-session link. It contributes to realized workload but never increases prescribed workload.
