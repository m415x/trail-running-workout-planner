# Readiness and Review Triage

## Training-response review
Human review of converging training signals and their context. It is an operational workflow, not a medical assessment.

## Convergence
The contemporaneous presence of independent, semantically compatible signals from multiple source domains. Convergence may increase review priority without implying causality or diagnosis.

## Contributor
A source-domain signal that explicitly participates in a triage classification. Contributors retain their source semantics and provenance.

## Review priority
An operational ordering that helps the coach decide which athlete to inspect first. `priority` is not clinical severity and is not injury probability.

## Unknown
A state in which the required observation is not known. Unknown is never coerced to zero, normal, success, or failure.

## Insufficient data
A state in which available evidence does not satisfy the rule's minimum requirements. It must remain visible to downstream consumers.

## Coverage
A description of how much of the expected evidence is known and evaluable. Coverage qualifies interpretation; it is not itself a physiological signal.

## Rule version
A stable identifier for the operational rule used to derive a signal. Versioning preserves traceability when heuristics evolve.

## Non-diagnostic signal
A derived observation intended to support coach review without claiming fatigue, injury, overreaching, overtraining syndrome, illness, or a probability of those outcomes.
