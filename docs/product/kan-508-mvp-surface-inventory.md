1. T2 may edit shared non-billing Coach page/action compositions, **not** Membership page/form or list exploration behavior.
2. T4 alone changes billing-specific view layouts and forms; never change `lib/memberships` economics/DB, temporal Terms, charge snapshots, revisions or prior-debt derivation for UX.
3. T6 must document a specific dataset/volume and a concrete list interaction problem before editing a list. Current groups, cohorts, planning and Sessions **do not** justify pagination on source evidence; Athlete list remains a question, not authorized scope expansion.
4. T5 may own an existing shared primitive adaptation; feature owners T2/T3/T4/T7 retain integration for their own surface. Avoid repeated modifications of one component across tasks.
5. T7 can replace literal RouteMapCard presentation labels and distinguish states; MapInner remains under existing KAN-569 contract. Keep behavior benchmark in T9.
6. T8 may address **bounded residual** accessibility defects; any cross-cutting new font preference, storage, or replacement of 120% baseline requires a new product/architecture decision.
